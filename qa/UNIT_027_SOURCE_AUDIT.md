## Mathematical repair and completion dossier

1. Solve the formal source exercise completely. A singular zero-cochain is a
   function on the points of `X`; the zero-cocycle condition says that its
   endpoint values agree on every path. There are no degree-minus-one
   coboundaries, so `H^0(X;R)` is exactly the module of functions on
   `[pt,X]=pi_0(X)`. For `f:X->Y`, pullback is precomposition with
   `pi_0(f):pi_0(X)->pi_0(Y)`.
2. Repair the type error in source line 5831. For a chosen point
   `x:pt->X`, the valid identity is `!_X composed with x=id_pt`, not `id_X`.
   Contravariance then gives `ev_x composed with const=id_R`, proving that
   `const:R->H^0(X;R)` is injective when `X` is nonempty.
3. Supply the omitted proof of the splitting lemma. Prove injectivity of
   `(r,pi)`, construct a preimage of `(a,c)` from any lift of `c`, and show
   that `pi` restricts to an isomorphism `ker(r)->C`. This also justifies the
   identification of `coker(const)` with `H^0(X,x;R)=ker(ev_x)`.
4. Move the sole margin note into reading order. The canonical quotient
   `coker(const)` is basepoint-independent, although its realization as the
   submodule `ker(ev_x)` can change when the chosen basepoint changes path
   component.
5. Repair source line 5847. For a path-connected space the typed conclusion
   is `tilde H^0(X;R)=0`; the phrase “for all k” cannot modify a degree-zero
   expression. If `X` is contractible, homotopy invariance additionally gives
   `tilde H^k(X;R)=0` in every nonnegative degree.
6. Preserve the topological pushout but replace its position-dependent Xy-pic
   rendering by a semantic arrow table and the pushout universal property.
7. Correct the principal cochain-level error in source lines 5867--5880. Let
   `C_*^U(X)=C_*(U)+C_*(V)` be the subcomplex generated by singular simplices
   whose images lie in `U` or `V`, and define the **small cochains** by
   `C_U^*(X;R)=Hom_R(C_*^U(X;R),R)`. This is naturally the kernel of the
   difference-of-restrictions map. It is not, in general, the claimed
   zero-extension subcomplex of `C^*(X;R)`: a non-small simplex may have small
   faces, so zero extension need not be preserved by the coboundary. The valid
   comparison map points in the opposite direction,
   `C^*(X;R)->C_U^*(X;R)`, by restriction.
8. Close the long comparison proof rather than relying only on a citation.
   Barycentric subdivision and the prism homotopy, together with the Lebesgue
   number lemma on each compact singular simplex, give a small-chain
   replacement and a chain homotopy inverse to
   `C_*^U(X)->C_*(X)`. Precomposition therefore makes restriction
   `C^*(X;R)->C_U^*(X;R)` a cochain-homotopy equivalence and hence a
   quasi-isomorphism for every coefficient ring `R`.
9. Prove the Mayer--Vietoris theorem from the degreewise short exact sequence
   of cochain complexes
   `0->C_U^*(X)->C^*(U) direct-sum C^*(V)->C^*(U intersection V)->0`,
   including the connecting morphism and exactness. The ordinary sequence is
   valid for every two-set open cover. Under the source's nonnegative-degree
   cokernel convention for reduced cohomology, the displayed reduced sequence
   beginning with zero requires `U intersection V` to be nonempty; otherwise
   one must use augmented reduced cohomology including degree minus one. State
   this hypothesis rather than silently asserting the over-broad version.
10. Make the sphere computation explicit. For the hemispherical cover,
    contractibility of the two pieces and
    `U intersection V` homotopy equivalent to `S^(n-1)` reduce the long exact
    sequence to
    `tilde H^(k-1)(S^(n-1);R) ~= tilde H^k(S^n;R)`. Starting from
    `tilde H^0(S^0;R)=R` gives `H^n(S^n;R)=R`; the same recurrence also gives
    zero in all other positive degrees and `H^0(S^n;R)=R` for `n>=1`.
